What is Sustainable Agriculture?

It's an integrated system of plant and animal production practices that will last over the long term.

Environmental Health

Protects biodiversity, reduces pollution, and builds healthy soil ecosystems for long-term productivity.

Economic Profitability

Reduces costs on chemical inputs and creates resilient farms that are profitable for farmers.

Social Equity

Supports fair working conditions and improves the quality of life for farming communities.

Key Sustainable Practices

1. Crop Rotation & Diversity

Growing a variety of crops in sequence improves soil health and breaks pest cycles naturally.

2. Water Conservation

Drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting reduce water waste by up to 60% while keeping crops healthy.

3. Agroforestry & Organic

Integrating trees with crops and avoiding synthetic chemicals enhances biodiversity and carbon capture.
